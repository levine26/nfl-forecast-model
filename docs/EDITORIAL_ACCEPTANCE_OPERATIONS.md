# Editorial publication acceptance: operational boundary

## Production incident, October 10, 2026

The current Week 5 15-game slate contained 0 applied provider HUMAN Reads. Its archived provider bundle, ChatGPT manifest, and frozen editorial roster still belonged to Week 4. Deterministic injury-led copy was published in every game, including formulaic `centers on TEAM: injury status` openings, while `editorial_finalizer.status` remained `healthy`. That previous status measures deterministic game-file completion and structural uniqueness, **not** verified professional NFL editorial quality.

The new `context_source_status.json.editorial_acceptance` is a separate slate-scoped, game-scoped acceptance diagnostic. It cannot be `structurally_eligible` if any game lacks provider-accepted prose, has mechanical fallback shapes, lacks distinct direct HTTPS publisher URL candidates, lacks both matchup teams, lacks exactly two paragraphs, duplicates long substantive prose, or has mismatched game IDs. Context status may remain operationally healthy while editorial acceptance reports degraded. Never present operational context success as human editorial success.

## IMPORTANT: this does not solve source verification

A syntactically credible article URL is NOT evidence that the publisher served the article or supports any claim. This diagnostic explicitly reports `article_resolution_verified=false` and `claim_support_verified=false`; full editorial acceptance additionally requires independent article retrieval, current-season roster/injury cross-checks, claim-to-passage records and a durable evidence receipt. Until then, no automation or operator should claim every game has two **verified** reports.

## Recovery

1. Confirm canonical Week, roster and provider artifact all contain identical game IDs.
2. Restore the autonomous Groq pipeline only after independent article and claim verification is operational, with bounded quota usage and current research.
3. For each failed matchup, preserve previously accepted provider copy; record its current-game fallback flag and follow the existing manifest protocol. Do not claim GitHub Actions autonomously invoked a ChatGPT consumer session.
4. Run focused checks, full-slate acceptance, official paragraph-2 sync, Pages publication and live-page inspection.
5. Never amend `F-ST-01-FROZEN-2026`, historical locks, official prediction probabilities or Candidate 4 to improve editorial acceptance.

## Interpretation of editorial status

`degraded`: at least one required HUMAN Read structural/provenance condition failed. `structurally_eligible`: the provisional structural audit passes for all games but is **not** a fact/source verification receipt. A launch-grade human Read requires the independent content and claim verification described above.
