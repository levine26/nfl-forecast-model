# Phase 1 Research Charter

## Mission

Decide, before observing new candidate performance, whether an ATS-specific distributional/quantile formulation is scientifically worth testing and exactly how it will be tested.

## Phase-1 admissible work

- read authoritative prior LevLine evidence;
- inspect current research/market infrastructure;
- review peer-reviewed and reproducible external work;
- audit market-data availability/PIT semantics;
- define mathematical targets and sign convention;
- preregister Q1/Q2/Q3, nulls, chronology, metrics, economics and uncertainty;
- create red-team/leakage tests;
- establish a Phase-2 handoff.

## Phase-1 prohibited work

Phase 1 may not:

- train Q1, Q2 or Q3;
- calculate new Q1/Q2/Q3 historical performance;
- inspect candidate-specific ATS hit rates;
- choose families/features/thresholds from target-period outcomes;
- optimize historical ROI;
- fit key-number parameters;
- use completed 2026 outcomes;
- modify F-ST or Sunday Signal forecasting behavior;
- implement Phase 2.

## Evidence hierarchy

External sources are classified as:

1. peer-reviewed evidence;
2. reproducible open-source implementation evidence;
3. strong technical practitioner evidence;
4. observable but opaque product/system evidence;
5. speculative hypothesis.

Reported third-party betting performance is never promoted above its reproducibility class merely because the reported return or ATS rate is high.

## Prior-evidence acceptance rule

Negative findings from Spread & Points Next-Generation are accepted. New work must not relabel rejected mean-margin/residual ideas as new ATS science.

Q1 is admissible because quantile loss/quantile targets are distinct from the previously rejected mean-residual objective. Q2 is admissible because discrete probability mass, conditional scale and push probabilities were not the target of C0. Q3 is admissible because it directly models the wagering outcome and is evaluated for incremental probability information beyond Q1/Q2.

## Decision standard

The Phase-1 decision is **TEST** the bounded Q1/Q2/Q3 program, not because there is evidence it will beat the market, but because:

- betting decisions are formally quantile/probability decisions;
- NFL margins are discrete and scoring-rule structured;
- the existing fixed Normal bridge does not model push/key-number mass;
- direct ATS probability heads target a distinct proper-scoring objective;
- all three claims can be falsified against market-based nulls under strict chronology.

The following ideas are deferred/rejected for V1 before results:

- unrestricted model search;
- mixtures/latent regimes;
- large player-state reconstruction;
- price-at-key latent fair-line modeling without priced historical data;
- conformal prediction as a primary ATS probability engine;
- any retrospective 2026 optimization.

## Reproducibility requirements for Phase 2

Every outer row must carry:

- game ID/season/week;
- market line and sign-convention fields;
- market source/provenance class;
- feature-as-of rule;
- outer fold;
- inner-selected specification identity;
- chronology proof fields;
- Q1/Q2/Q3 version IDs;
- calibration/blend version IDs;
- no-lookahead assertions.

The experiment registry must be machine-readable and deterministic.

## Governance

A favorable historical ATS percentage cannot override a proper-score failure against the relevant market null. A calibration-only improvement must be labeled calibration-only. A football-incremental claim requires improvement over M2, not merely M0. Historical development cannot promote production; Phase 4 prospective shadow remains a separate authorization gate.
