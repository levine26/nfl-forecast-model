# Post-Sunday data-source recommendations

These are research priorities, not assumed access or Sunday dependencies.

| Source | Highest-value variables | Historical/PIT value | Live value | Identity/licensing limits | Recommended use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FantasyPoints Data Suite | routes, route share, alignment, target depth, coverage and personnel context | Confirm exact historical depth and publication-time fields before purchase | Potentially strong for same-week route/role state | Paid; redistribution and stable-ID terms must be verified | First vendor trial if it supplies timestamped all-player routes and injury/workload history |
| Sports Info Solutions | charted blocking, box/front, run concept, coverage and route detail | Potentially strong mechanism research if historical release chronology is available | Valuable for box-count and coverage-route mechanisms | Paid; field definitions, latency, IDs and redistribution must be audited | Best fit for the two highest-value mechanism gaps after a scoped sample audit |
| PFF Developer API | routes, snaps, alignments, grades and injury/participation context | Potential multi-season depth; PIT publication chronology must be confirmed | Useful if current-week fields arrive before forecast lock | Paid; access scope and redistribution unclear | Compare with FantasyPoints on route completeness, latency and IDs before contracting |
| nflverse | play-by-play, rosters, snaps and reproducible public history | Excellent for lagged football history | PBP/snaps can support conservative lagged proxies | 2023+ participation is postseason-delayed and is not live route evidence | Retain as the open baseline and validation source; never relabel participation as same-season live routes |
| NFL Next Gen Stats public surfaces | methodology and selected aggregate tracking metrics | Useful for mechanism design; public historical coverage varies | Limited public live player fields | Raw tracking is not available to LevLine | Use methodology and available aggregates only; do not claim raw tracking access |
| Official NFL/team injury and depth reporting | status, activation, starter and limited-workload evidence | Historical reconstruction is incomplete | Highest-value current personnel evidence | Source timing and stable player identity must be captured | Expand the structured evidence collector and immutable capture archive |

Priority order after Sunday:

1. Stronger live route participation and historical point-in-time injury/workload data.
2. Box-count × run-concept and coverage-shell × route/alignment mechanism trials.
3. Bayesian/event-driven role-regime modeling after sufficient prospective labels exist.
4. Hierarchical calibration and market microstructure/book-leader experiments.
5. Alternate-line/tail-distribution research and a separately preregistered LevLine game-state information-transfer experiment.
