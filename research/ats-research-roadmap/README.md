## Governing interpretation

The accumulated evidence does **not** support another round of threshold tuning or a cosmetically renamed model on the same information set. The central research question is now:

> Does any information available before kickoff add stable predictive information beyond a strong contemporaneous sportsbook market state, and if so, can that information be translated into a calibrated discrete outcome distribution without losing the gain?

Three existing prospective programs remain distinct and must not be silently merged or renamed:

1. `FV2-PROS-M1-MARKETSTATE-01` — dynamic multi-book market-state research at T-120, including a separately frozen T-60 future-market intermediate target.
2. `FV3-PROS-KMASS-01` — prospective key-mass distribution program centered on the T-120 market spread.
3. `FV2-PROS-M2-QBDELTA-01` — prospective player/QB information-delta research, with incomplete/partial capture explicitly preserved as missing where unavailable.

The current roadmap creates no new production candidate and authorizes no outcome-driven rescue of a rejected experiment.
