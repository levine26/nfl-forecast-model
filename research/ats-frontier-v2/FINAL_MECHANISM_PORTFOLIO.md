# FINAL MECHANISM PORTFOLIO

Phase 3 reduces the Phase-1 shortlist to the smallest portfolio supported by qualified data. This is a data/scientific decision made before candidate performance exists.

## Historical Phase-4 candidates

### `FV2-HIST-M3-DSSM-01`

Mechanism: chronology-safe hierarchical dynamic football state expressed only as a shrinkage correction around the sportsbook market benchmark.

Status: `FROZEN__PHASE4_ELIGIBLE`.

Why retained: the core lagged PBP/QB state is data-qualified, the state-space representation is materially different from fixed rolling windows, and the market is explicitly the null/baseline rather than an afterthought.

### `FV2-HIST-M4-DMARGIN-01`

Mechanism: analytically tail-safe market-centered Student-t integer-margin PMF with training-only conditional scale and compact scoring-lattice mass adjustments at 0, |3| and |7|.

Status: `FROZEN__PHASE4_ELIGIBLE`.

Why retained: Q2 V1 failed numerically before a valid performance test; V2 directly fixes support/tail/bin-integration failure and is evaluated as a probability-representation experiment, not presumed alpha.

## Mechanisms excluded from historical Phase 4

- M1 historical dynamic market state: blocked by free-data coverage/cadence/provenance gate.
- M2 broad player-state delta: no unified 2022–2025 PIT availability contract.
- M2 historical 2025-only personnel experiment: excluded because one narrow season/source does not justify a general historical candidate and event concentration would be severe.
- forecast combination: not an independent candidate; remains closed until independently validated survivors demonstrate complementary residual information.

## Portfolio size decision

Phase 4 has **two** historical candidates, not three merely to hit a planning target. The Phase-3 mandate explicitly allows candidates to die on scientific/data grounds. Creating a third historical model after M1/M2 failed their required data scope would reintroduce algorithm-first search.
