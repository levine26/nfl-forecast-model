# QB STATE PIT AUDIT

QB is a separately governed M2 component.

## Qualified pieces

- **QB ability:** lagged prior-game play data are chronology-safe.
- **Backup/replacement quality:** may be estimated from information strictly prior to the target game.
- **2025 final practice state before T-120:** available only within the already-qualified narrow 2025 reconstruction contract.
- **2025+ depth-chart snapshots:** nflverse supplies ISO8601 load timestamps after the post-2024 source change, permitting PIT assignment.

## Not qualified generically

- A final game's starter identity cannot be backfilled to an earlier prediction horizon.
- Same-game snaps/participation cannot establish pregame starter probability.
- Final inactive lists cannot be used at T-120 unless a source proves their release before T-120.
- No unified 2022–2025 player/QB availability state is authorized; the existing harmonization attempt failed closed.

## Phase-3 constraint

Any future `QB starter probability` must be conditioned only on observations whose publication/observation timestamp is at or before the candidate's frozen prediction horizon. Missing or conflicting evidence must remain explicit.
