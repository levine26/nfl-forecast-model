## Cross-family failure pattern

1. **Market dominance is persistent.** When LevLine is asked to predict with the same broad public football information the market sees, it does not beat a strong market null.
2. **Tiny historical gains are unstable.** Adaptive examples show one to three switched games can create an attractive headline while providing little independent evidence.
3. **Architecture is not a substitute for information.** Drive simulation, quantiles, hurdle classification and residual stacking all fail if their inputs do not contain conditional information beyond market state.
4. **Numerical validity matters.** Q2 proves that a theoretically appealing distribution can be unusable if support/tails are not validated before scoring.
5. **The next program must distinguish information from representation.** M1/M2 target potentially new information; M3/M4 improve state/uncertainty representation and therefore face a higher burden to demonstrate incrementality.
