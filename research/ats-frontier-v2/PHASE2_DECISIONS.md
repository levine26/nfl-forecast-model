# PHASE 2 DECISIONS — APPENDIX TO DECISION_LOG

The frozen Phase-1 `DECISION_LOG.md` is preserved verbatim. These Phase-2 decisions are additive and become the next decision-log block at closeout.

## D016 — M1 remains partial pending real historical coverage
`FRONTIER-M1-DYNAMIC-MARKET-STATE` = `PARTIALLY_QUALIFIED`. Provider documentation establishes PIT-capable historical structures, but full season × book × fixed-horizon coverage is not yet empirically established.

## D017 — PropLine is prospective-only for the historical Frontier program
Do not use PropLine as pre-2026 historical M1 evidence; its current first-party documentation states that the archive starts in April 2026 for this program's purposes.

## D018 — M2 narrowed, not killed
`FRONTIER-M2-PLAYER-STATE-DELTA` = `PARTIALLY_QUALIFIED`. Lagged ability/replacement quality and narrowly qualified timestamped personnel states are legitimate; the injury-source discontinuity and failed cross-year harmonization prohibit a unified broad state.

## D019 — M3 core advances independently of rich personnel state
`FRONTIER-M3-HIERARCHICAL-STATE` = `DATA_QUALIFIED` for core lagged team/QB state. Current-week rich personnel inputs remain governed by M2.

## D020 — M4 advances as representation only
`FRONTIER-M4-DISCRETE-MARGIN-V2` = `DATA_QUALIFIED` for numerical/data feasibility. No predictive-alpha claim is made.

## D021 — Historical odds remains the only potentially justified paid-data category
If and only if free reconstruction is later shown scientifically inadequate and the user explicitly authorizes a bounded qualification pull, The Odds API remains the first commercial fallback and SportsDataIO the alternative. No general football-stat purchase is recommended.

## D022 — Completed-2026 and production firewalls remain intact
Phase 2 uses zero completed-2026 outcomes for candidate design and makes no production forecasting changes.

## D023 — Paid historical odds purchase is deferred behind a mandatory free-first audit
The free/open-source exhaustion search found real historical market artifacts across the intended era. No purchase is recommended now. M1 becomes `PARTIALLY_QUALIFIED__FREE_FIRST_RECONSTRUCTION_REQUIRED` until season × game × book × horizon coverage is measured.

## D024 — Public visibility is not a redistribution license
A public GitHub repository or release may be inspected and cited as research evidence, but raw data is not copied into LevLine merely because it is publicly accessible. Free-source records receive a separate rights class: `LICENSE_CLEAR`, `LICENSE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, or `NO_REDISTRIBUTION_ASSUMED`.

## D025 — Public historical API caches are accepted only with row-level timing proof
Provider-origin cached historical odds may be used as PIT evidence only where authoritative kickoff and source timestamp demonstrate that the observation is strictly pre-kick. Equal-to-kickoff and post-kick rows are rejected. A sparse daily snapshot is not relabeled as a frozen exact horizon unless it actually satisfies the at-or-before selector.

## D026 — Free data may narrow M1 only on coverage/provenance grounds
Phase 3 may constrain M1 to free-supported eras/horizons before any candidate performance is inspected. It may not choose a source, horizon or subset because ATS, ROI or predictive results appear favorable.

## D027 — Commercial fallback requires an explicit empirical deficiency
The Odds API/SportsDataIO may be reconsidered only after the free audit records the exact missing coverage/provenance needed for the frozen M1 mechanism and the user explicitly authorizes the bounded purchase. This decision log does not authorize spending.
