# DAVID SASSER REVIEW

David Sasser was a required named review target because prior LevLine discussions repeatedly referenced his public modeling work.

## What is publicly verifiable now

- `davidsasser.com` currently presents Sasser's broader machine-learning / engineering portfolio.
- The public football projection page located during Phase 1 is a **college-football** (`/cfb`) product showing projected scores/lines, opening/current market information and ATS record context.
- Public GitHub material under the David Sasser identity includes football/data-science projects, including NFL Big Data Bowl-related work, but Phase 1 did **not** locate a reproducible current NFL ATS model with enough source/method detail to audit end-to-end.

## What is not justified

Phase 1 found no defensible basis to state that Sasser currently uses a particular NFL architecture, proprietary injury model, market weighting formula, or training procedure. Those details are unknown unless directly documented. The program therefore does not infer them from output screens.

## Transferable public concepts

The defensible concepts visible in the football projection product are:

1. publish a model projection alongside the opening/current market rather than hiding the benchmark;
2. present projected score and projected spread coherently;
3. track ATS record prospectively;
4. expose market movement context to the user.

These are product/governance ideas, not proof of an exploitable forecasting method.

## Research disposition

David Sasser remains a useful practitioner comparison, but the current public evidence is **insufficient to elevate any hidden Sasser methodology into the Frontier shortlist**. Future phases may revisit only if a reproducible public repository, methodology note or directly documented model becomes available. Do not invent proprietary content.
