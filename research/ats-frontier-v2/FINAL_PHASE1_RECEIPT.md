## Phase-1 scientific conclusion

The best explanation for repeated LevLine failure is not insufficient algorithm complexity. The sportsbook market is a strong dynamic information aggregator, while prior LevLine challengers largely reused public football state, compressed the market into too little state, and sometimes produced attractive ATS headlines from very few switched games.

Phase 1 therefore freezes four mechanism families for outcome-blind Phase-2 data qualification:

1. `FRONTIER-M1-DYNAMIC-MARKET-STATE` — latent multi-book market state from timestamped spread, side price, moneyline, total, quote age, disagreement, breadth, velocity and lead/lag structure.
2. `FRONTIER-M2-PLAYER-STATE-DELTA` — expected lineup/QB value change minus contemporaneous market-implied change, explicitly separating ability, participation probability, expected role and replacement quality.
3. `FRONTIER-M3-HIERARCHICAL-STATE` — sample-efficient hierarchical latent offense/defense/QB/unit strength with process noise, partial pooling and regime capacity instead of fixed rolling-window compression.
4. `FRONTIER-M4-DISCRETE-MARGIN-V2` — a new tail-safe integer-margin probability representation with adaptive/unbounded support, correct bin integration, explicit push mass and conditional key-number/tail structure. This is a representation experiment, not a presumed alpha source.

Forecast combination is not an independent candidate. It may reopen only if independently validated survivors later demonstrate complementary residual information.

## Data conclusion

The highest-value data opportunity is timestamped historical multi-book odds and price movement. The Odds API and SportsDataIO are the leading Phase-2 sample-qualification targets because their public documentation describes historical snapshots/revision history suited to reconstructing market state. No purchase was made in Phase 1.

Player-state data is the principal feasibility risk. nflverse supplies strong public PBP/roster/snap infrastructure, but injury/depth-chart source continuity and true as-of history cannot be assumed. Phase 2 is allowed to eliminate M2 rather than fill missing PIT state with ex-post information.
