## `CONSTANT_SCALE_KEY` representation

Status: **`FUTURE_VERSION_HYPOTHESIS_ONLY`**.

Accepted Phase-4 ablation evidence shows that a constant-scale Student-t integer-margin representation with frozen mass offsets at `0`, `|3|`, and `|7|` produced a primary-score improvement of `-0.08271839184340689` versus the strong null and slightly outperformed the rejected full M4 candidate by `0.00004669270045401389`.

This is scientifically interesting evidence about NFL discrete scoring representation. It is **not** a Phase-5 survivor because `CONSTANT_SCALE_KEY` was preregistered as an ablation, not as the frozen candidate identity.

It may not be renamed or backdated as `M4-V2`, `FV2-HIST-M4-KEY`, or an equivalent survivor inside this program phase.

Any future test requires all of:

- a new candidate identity;
- a new preregistration written before new target evidence is inspected;
- fresh scientific/governance contracts;
- a legitimate future validation path that does not recycle the 2022–2025 development result as pristine confirmation;
- no completed-2026 retrospective contamination in candidate design if those outcomes are intended for later prospective evaluation.
