# Uncertainty and Stopping Rule

No early success stop. Formal evaluation earliest: end of 2027 regular season.

Minimum evidence: at least 300 eligible games, 20 distinct NFL season-week blocks, two seasons, and at least 64 eligible games in each represented season. If the threshold is not satisfied at the preregistered endpoint, status is `INCONCLUSIVE_DATA_SHORTFALL`; any continued prospective collection must preserve the frozen candidate and requires governance documentation, not retuning.

At evaluation use paired candidate-null differences and 10,000 week-block bootstrap resamples with seed `20260924`; report 95% percentile interval, per-week and per-season contributions, leave-one-week-out deltas, missingness, and calibration stability. A single week cannot determine eligibility because every leave-one-week-out mean must remain favorable.
