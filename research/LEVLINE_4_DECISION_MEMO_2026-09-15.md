# LevLine 4 scientific decision memo

**Decision date:** 2026-09-15 America/Los_Angeles  
**Program:** `LEVLINE-4-INFORMATION-RESIDUAL-V2`  
**Status:** research conclusion and infrastructure disposition; **not a production promotion**

## Executive decision

No currently available evidence justifies declaring a new production LevLine 4 probability architecture superior to `F-ST-01-FROZEN-2026`.

The strongest scientifically defensible next-generation architecture is still a **market-first residual architecture**, but the proprietary residuals are presently **zero by default** because expected-lineup, richer QB replacement state, personnel-conditioned matchup effects, market-path effects and bookmaker-quality weights have not yet earned nonzero coefficients under point-in-time, same-horizon, paired evaluation.

In compact form, the research target is:

`logit(P_h) = logit(Market_h) + δ_existing + δ_lineup + δ_QB + δ_market_path + δ_matchup`

where every `δ` other than already frozen incumbent structure is zero unless a separately preregistered candidate establishes stable incremental value beyond the same-horizon market and incumbent. Calibration, if used, is an independently validated layer rather than an automatic transformation.

This is not a negative result. The research supports several **capability/infrastructure improvements** that should exist even before they affect official probabilities:

- immutable exact-horizon market capture and market-path state;
- raw official inactive archival;
- a governed inactive-information event ledger that separates football-state shock from market response;
- a governed structured-beat observation schema that allows factual extraction but forbids LLM probability judgment;
- qualified source/identity work that preserves explicit unknown states and refuses retrospective fabrication;
- a frozen H1-H10 information-residual preregistration.

`F-ST-01-FROZEN-2026` remains the official forecast of record at T-120. No work in this program authorizes mutation of historical locks, frozen coefficients, prior receipts or production probabilities.

## 3. Objective-function reconciliation

The original LevLine 4 preregistration made Brier/probability quality the primary selection criterion. A later September 12 recommendation elevated sustainable OOS winner accuracy for a proposed pick layer after the component-resolved candidate reached 744 correct versus 741 for F-ST and 735 for market on the current keyed historical audit.

Those are different scientific claims and must remain separate:

1. **Probability-model superiority** requires proper-score and calibration evidence against the same-horizon market, with winner accuracy reported explicitly.
2. **A discrete pick-layer hypothesis** can study winner accuracy, but it cannot be represented as a superior probability model merely because more winners were correct.
3. The historical component-resolved gain is small and concentrated enough that the later selective-error-correction work did not establish a stable switch rule.

For `LEVLINE-4-INFORMATION-RESIDUAL-V2`, Brier is therefore frozen as the primary metric for probability corrections; log loss, calibration and winner accuracy are mandatory secondary metrics. This does not rewrite the September 12 historical recommendation. It defines the objective before the new H1-H10 outcomes exist.

## 5. Peer-reviewed literature synthesis

The literature review supports four high-level conclusions.

### 5.1 Betting markets are a high bar

NFL-specific work repeatedly finds betting lines difficult for statistical systems and human experts to beat as forecasts. This is direct evidence against qualitative overrides and against treating an intuitively important injury or matchup feature as proprietary edge before conditioning on the market.

### 5.2 Information timing matters

NFL sequential-line work finds later lines contain/encompass information from earlier states, and newer NFL work finds systematic variation in line movement with game visibility. Cross-sport player-absence research likewise suggests roster information can matter at an earlier price and disappear after the market absorbs it. Therefore LevLine must evaluate lineup/QB information against the market that existed at the **same horizon**.

### 5.3 Player and state models are legitimate measurement tools, not automatic edges

NFL player-value research, Bayesian state-space work and hierarchical methods provide defensible ways to estimate player/unit state with shrinkage. They do not show that such state remains orthogonal to a contemporaneous market probability.

### 5.4 Forecast combination and calibration require restraint

Proper-scoring, calibration and forecast-combination theory all warn that estimated weights can add variance and that combinations of calibrated forecasts can themselves become miscalibrated. The default should therefore be the smallest model and the strongest shrinkage compatible with reproducible incremental evidence.

NFL-specific evidence is prioritized. Soccer/NBA/AFL evidence is used only for transferable methodology or to motivate a hypothesis; no cross-sport effect size is imported into LevLine.

## 6. NFL Beat comparison

NFL Beat is useful here only as an **information-class inventory**. Its public matchup surface foregrounds HC/OC/DC/QB state, personnel context, matchup analysis and beat intelligence. That is enough to motivate research questions about structured football state.

It is not evidence that NFL Beat's picks are statistically superior, and no proprietary implementation or qualitative pick logic is copied.

The LevLine translation is deliberately narrower:

- source/timestamped expected starter;
- expected snap/role tier;
- position or OL-combination change;
- return-to-full-role / limited-role state;
- coach-confirmed personnel change;
- scheme/personnel usage change;
- uncertainty state.

An LLM may extract those factual observations into a governed schema. It may not decide that an observation is worth a particular number of percentage points, generate a directional edge, or override the official forecast.

## 8. Ablation plan if dependencies eventually clear

The frozen sequence is deliberately small:

- **A:** same-horizon market only
- **B:** A + existing F-ST/PURE structure
- **C:** B + expected-lineup residual
- **D:** C + separate QB state
- **E:** D + market-path state
- **F:** E + only the preregistered personnel-conditioned interactions

Each candidate gets one execution under its frozen identity. Failed candidates cannot be rescued by modifying features, thresholds, interactions, weights, model family or hyperparameters under the same name.

The core probability criterion is paired Brier improvement on identical games with season-week block uncertainty, corroborated by log loss and calibration. Winner accuracy is always reported. Season stability and concentration diagnostics are mandatory. Subgroups are diagnostic unless separately preregistered before outcomes.

## 9. What does the market already capture?

The evidence strongly implies that the market already captures a substantial fraction of:

- public injury and inactive information;
- QB availability news;
- conventional team strength;
- widely observed recent performance;
- public coaching/system narratives;
- obvious matchup information;
- and, increasingly through the week, information that was present in earlier prices.

That does not imply perfect efficiency. It means those facts cannot be counted a second time without residual evidence.

## 10. What proprietary information could remain?

The plausible proprietary opportunity is **measurement and timing**, not narrative conviction:

1. a more accurate expected-lineup state than the market had at a given moment;
2. explicit starter probability and replacement-quality uncertainty for QB and other high-leverage roles;
3. the *surprise* between prior expected state and official inactive/role confirmation;
4. cross-book path structure, dispersion, breadth and stale-book disagreement at the same horizon;
5. tightly specified personnel-conditioned interactions that aggregate team efficiency misses;
6. uncertainty-aware shrinkage that suppresses weak or low-provenance residuals.

Each remains a hypothesis until its incremental information survives same-horizon evaluation.

## 11. Direct answers to the required questions

### Does lineup intelligence add value?

**Not yet demonstrated beyond the market.** There is enough evidence to justify prospective measurement infrastructure, but not enough qualified historical PIT data to fit the proposed lineup residual honestly.

### Does QB state add value?

**QB state is clearly important to football and market behavior; incremental forecast value beyond the same-horizon market is not yet demonstrated.** Treat QB separately, collect starter probability/replacement state prospectively, and do not assign editorial point values.

### Do personnel-conditioned matchups add value?

**Unknown and currently blocked.** Generic interaction attempts did not establish value. The more principled personnel-conditioned version should not run until expected personnel state is itself validated.

### Does market-path information add value?

**Scientifically plausible, operationally measurable, but not yet demonstrated as an outcome residual.** The current strict-PIT path ledger is the correct substrate. Do not infer a steam/reversal betting rule from 2026 results.

### Does bookmaker weighting improve the market prior?

**Not established for LevLine.** Cross-book quality differences exist in peer-reviewed work, but LevLine lacks qualified pre-2026 individual-book PIT history for outcome-safe NFL weighting. Continue frozen prospective consensus/de-vig comparisons.

### Does a structural-break model help?

**No existing LevLine result supports generic dynamic strength as an improvement.** A new structural-break model remains scientifically distinct only when tied to predeclared PIT football events. It is lower priority than lineup/QB/market-path work.

### Does any tested combination materially beat the incumbent?

**No combination currently satisfies the evidence standard for a superior production probability model.** Component-resolved historical accuracy is interesting but did not establish a stable production switch rule or probability-score superiority. Existing generic player, interaction, latent-strength and dynamic-Elo lanes do not earn promotion.

### What is the simplest winning architecture?

There is **no validated nonzero new residual yet**. The simplest defensible architecture today is the existing market/F-ST core plus research-only point-in-time intelligence infrastructure. If H1/H2/H4 later validate, the target should remain a market prior with a small regularized residual, not a large football-first model.

### What should LevLine 4.0 actually be?

At the current evidence state, **LevLine 4.0 should not be promoted as a new production probability model**. The scientifically defensible “LevLine 4” work today is a capability layer: exact-horizon market state, expected-lineup/QB capture, inactive-event surprise, structured factual beat extraction, provenance, uncertainty and conditional-scenario infrastructure held behind a production firewall.

If prospective evidence later proves one or more residuals, production LevLine 4 should be the smallest subset of those residuals that materially and stably improves the same-horizon market/incumbent. If none does, LevLine should remain market/F-ST based. That would be a successful research conclusion, not a failure.

## 12. Operational complexity and failure modes

The largest scientific risks are not ordinary model bugs. They are:

- silent use of post-event/post-kickoff state;
- heterogeneous historical availability semantics presented as one feature;
- inferring healthy/active from missing source rows;
- mapping a player to a team without proving game-day membership;
- double counting public injury/QB information already priced by the market;
- learning book weights or market-path rules from completed 2026 outcomes;
- feature mining tiny disagreement or injury subgroups;
- conflating accuracy gains with calibrated probability improvement;
- allowing extraction confidence to become a directional edge;
- rescuing failed source/model candidates by changing gates after observation.

The newly added schemas and preregistration are designed to fail closed against those errors.
